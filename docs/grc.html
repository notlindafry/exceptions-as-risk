2026 objectives progress

Automate 80% of all evidence collection
Evidence collected automatically is 80% complete — the payments team stood up a collector for their own controls, retiring a batch of manual pulls and returning audit hours to higher-value review.
Controls carrying an automated test is 55% complete — the first self-testing controls went into production, surfacing two configuration drifts before they reached a customer.
Auto-draft 50% of new risk intake
New risk records auto-drafted for review is 60% complete — intake now opens from an AI-generated draft, compressing the time from identification to a scored, owned risk and returning analyst capacity to judgment work.
Findings auto-routed to their control is 69% complete — findings now attach to the control they affect automatically, so none wait unrouted on manual filing.
